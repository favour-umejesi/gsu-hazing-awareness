Understanding Hazing

Hazing Statistics

Hazing is more common than most people realize, and no level of hazing is allowed or acceptable.

National Research

National hazing statistics

Hazing is not a challenge unique to any one campus. It is common in sports, clubs, and student organizations across the nation.

55%

of students in clubs and organizations have been hazed.

91%

of students who were hazed did not realize it was hazing.

95%

of students who were hazed did not report it.

1 in 10

students who were hazed labeled it as hazing.

74%

of student-athletes experience some form of hazing.

Presence of Alcohol

Alcohol and hazing

Alcohol is often involved in hazing incidents.

71%

of students have witnessed alcohol-related hazing.

47%

of student-athletes report hazing that involved drinking games.

82%

of hazing deaths are alcohol-related.

Before College

It often starts earlier than you think

Many students experience hazing even before college begins.

1.5M

high school students experience hazing each year.

47%

of college students were already hazed back in high school.

25%

of those hazed were first hazed before the age of 13.

Sources

Where this data comes from

National data was primarily sourced from the national study Hazing in View: College Students at Risk, conducted by Elizabeth Allan, Ph.D. and Mary Madden, Ph.D.

  • Hoover and Pollard, 2000
  • Allan, E.J. and Madden, M., 2008
  • WITH US Center for Bystander Intervention, 2020
