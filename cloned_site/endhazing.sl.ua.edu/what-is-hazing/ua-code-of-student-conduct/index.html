GSU Code of Student Conduct

The standards by which students are expected to conduct themselves at Grambling State University.

The information below reflects the Office of Student Conduct as described on Grambling State University's Student Conduct (Judicial Affairs) page. The official rules are published in the Revised Code of Student Conduct handbook (PDF).

Purpose

Purpose of the Office of Student Conduct

The purpose of the Office of Student Conduct is to ensure that students will not be deprived of life, liberty, or property; to administer a fair student disciplinary process that adheres to prescribed standards; and to establish guidelines and rules to ensure that students maintain appropriate behavior at the University and off-campus. The Code of Student Conduct creates an expectation of behavior that the Institution deems acceptable. It is each student's responsibility to adhere to the policies and standards of conduct prescribed by the University, the Board of Supervisors for the University of Louisiana System, and applicable law.

Goals

What the Code aims to do

Responsibility

Promote student development by challenging and supporting students in owning individual responsibility.

Growth

Use educational strategies and disciplinary counseling as catalysts for self-understanding and personal growth.

Civility

Enforce a system characterized by accountability, compassion, and increased civility.

Safe climate

Promote a safe, orderly, and positive University climate through programming, training, and intervention.
