Understanding Hazing

GSU Hazing Policy

Grambling State University is committed to providing an environment that is free from all forms of hazing.

Grambling State University is committed to providing an environment for students, employees, and campus visitors that is free from all forms of hazing. Hazing violates both policy and law and may lead to personal liability for the results of such behavior.

What the Policy Covers

The GSU Hazing Policy addresses these areas

Prohibition on Hazing

All forms of hazing are strictly prohibited, against any individual or organization in the campus community.

Reporting Hazing

Every member of the community has a duty to promptly report good-faith concerns about hazing.

Prohibition on Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports hazing in good faith is prohibited.
