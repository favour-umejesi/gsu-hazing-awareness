Student Engagement & Leadership

The Office of Student Engagement and Leadership (OSEL) is the primary campus unit for student involvement, organizations, programming, and leadership development.

Counseling Center

If you or someone you know has been hazed and is struggling mentally, the Counseling Center can help. Individual appointments are available Monday through Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m.

Location: Foster-Johnson, Room 113
Phone: 318-274-3163 / 318-274-3277
