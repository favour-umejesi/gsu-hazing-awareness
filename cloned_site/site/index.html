The Reality of Hazing

Knowing the numbers is the first step to change

55%

of students in clubs and organizations have been hazed.

91%

of students who were hazed did not realize it was hazing.

95%

of students who were hazed did not report it to campus officials.

74%

of student-athletes experience some form of hazing.

Sources: Allan & Madden, Hazing in View: College Students at Risk (2008); Hoover & Pollard (2000).
