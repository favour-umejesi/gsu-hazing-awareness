Prevention in Practice

Four steps anyone can take

01

Know the signs

Learn what isolation, pressure, and secrecy can look like in a club or team, even when it is framed as "tradition."

02

Set the standard

Make a culture of respect explicit. Leaders set the tone and members hold each other accountable.

03

Step in early

Speak up before things escalate. A quiet word or a question early can change the outcome.

04

Report it

Use the G Safe app or call. Reporting protects students and keeps the whole community safe.

Why Prevention Matters

Hazing is more common, and more hidden, than most realize

1.5M

high school students experience hazing each year.

47%

of college students were already hazed back in high school.

25%

of those hazed were first hazed before the age of 13.

71%

of students have witnessed alcohol-related hazing.

Sources: Allan & Madden, Hazing in View: College Students at Risk (2008); Hoover & Pollard (2000).
